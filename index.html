Panther Pad Interactive

Privacy Policy

The game studio of Panther Pad LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company.

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Panther Pad Interactive handles information in our mobile games. Panther Pad Interactive is the game studio of Panther Pad LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company owned by Christopher Allan Roberge, of Groveton, New Hampshire, USA. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Panther Pad Interactive and Panther Pad LLC.

Games this policy covers

This policy applies to these games, which we plan to offer first on Google Play and later on the Apple App Store:

  • Kente Match
  • Coconut Dash
  • Tropical Merge
  • Sunset Stack
  • Pearl Drop

The games are available in English, Haitian Creole, French, and Spanish. Sunset Stack and Pearl Drop are made for children. All five games follow the same children’s privacy rules described here.

The short version

We do not collect personal information. We do not use ads, tracking, or analytics. The games do not have accounts or chat. Settings and progress stay on your device. You can delete that information by uninstalling the game.

What we collect — and what we don’t

We do not collect personal information. The games do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photo, location, or any other information that identifies you or your child. They do not create accounts. They do not include chat or any way to share information with other players. They do not use advertising identifiers.

Settings and progress stay on the device. Each game may save things like language choice, sound and accessibility settings, and game progress in tables stored on the device (GameSalad tables). This information is not sent to us, to GameSalad, or to anyone else. If you uninstall the game or clear the game’s data in your device settings, those saves are deleted. We cannot see, recover, or restore them.

We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics. The games do not contain ads. They do not include analytics or crash-reporting tools that send information to us or to a third party. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have it. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics software in the games. We do not enable ad networks or analytics, including GameAnalytics, in our builds.

Purchases

The games do not include in-game stores, loot boxes, or advertising. Some games may be sold at a one-time price or offer a one-time unlock. Any such purchase is always made through Google Play or the App Store, and a parent must approve it (a parent gate). We never see or store payment details. The store handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

The games do not need an internet connection to play, except where the store itself handles a purchase.

Children’s privacy

We designed these games with children and families in mind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, including children under 13.

Because we do not collect personal information, a child does not need to give us any personal information in order to play. Parents do not need to create an account or give us contact details for a child to use the games.

This approach is intended to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play’s Families policy, and Apple’s Kids Category rules. If we ever change how the games work in a way that would collect personal information from children, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting that information.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example, by emailing us), please contact us at the address below. We will delete that information.

How parents can delete information

Game settings and progress exist only on the device. To delete them:

  1. Uninstall the game, or
  2. Use your device’s settings to clear the game’s stored data.

That removes the local saves. We have no copy.

If you have emailed us, you may ask us to delete that email by contacting us.

The GameSalad engine and the app stores

These games are built with GameSalad and prepared for the stores through GameSalad’s publishing service. They are then listed on Google Play and, later, the Apple App Store.

What GameSalad has said about published games. GameSalad staff have stated on the official GameSalad forums that the GameSalad engine no longer collects data from published games, except when a developer turns on ad networks or a tool called GameAnalytics. We do not enable ad networks or analytics, including GameAnalytics, in our builds.

GameSalad’s own privacy policy (last updated May 14, 2021) describes data collected on GameSalad’s website and developer platform, including tools such as Google Analytics. That policy covers people who use GameSalad’s site and tools, not players of these games. GameSalad also uses a crash reporter called BugSplat in GameSalad Creator, the software used to make games. Public GameSalad documentation describes BugSplat as a Creator tool, not as something included in published games.

The app stores. Google Play and the Apple App Store operate under their own privacy policies. They may collect information about installs, purchases, and device-level crash or performance reports. That collection is controlled by Google or Apple, not by us. Google and Apple may also give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts, crash reports, and sales totals. These statistics do not identify any player.

We do not run our own servers for these games, and the games are not designed to send player information to us.

This policy on the web

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change is material — especially a change that would involve collecting personal information — we will provide a clearer notice, such as an in-game message or a notice on the page where this policy is posted, before the change takes effect.
